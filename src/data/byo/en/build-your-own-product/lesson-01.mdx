## Why it matters

A useful build starts with a clear decision. Start with a specific recurring pain instead of a feature-shaped solution.

## Build it

Make the smallest version visible, ask what a real person needs next, and leave the surrounding system simpler than you found it.

## Practice

Write down one concrete change you can make today. Keep the scope small enough to finish before your next interruption.

## Next

Carry that evidence into the next lesson instead of adding work by instinct.
